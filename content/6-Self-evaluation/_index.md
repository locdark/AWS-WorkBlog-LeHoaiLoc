During my participation in the FIRST CLOUD AI JOURNEY (FCAJ) BOOTCAMP from June 22, 2026, to August 15, 2026, I had the opportunity to learn and work in a professional environment while applying the knowledge I gained at university to a real-world project. The program helped me gain deeper insight into the development and deployment workflow of an application system on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud platform, thereby enhancing my understanding of the role and responsibilities of a Cloud Engineer in an enterprise.

Throughout the internship, my team and I developed "Foodie Recipes" – a platform designed to help users search, manage, and receive recipe recommendations powered by Artificial Intelligence. The project was built and deployed on Amazon Web Services (AWS). I actively participated in analyzing project requirements, designing system architecture, and deploying and configuring AWS services including Amazon VPC, Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, AWS IAM, Amazon RDS, Amazon Rekognition, Amazon Bedrock, AWS Secrets Manager, and Amazon CloudWatch. Additionally, I contributed to building the management system, designing system architecture diagrams, supporting testing activities, creating technical documentation, and finalizing reports as requested by our mentor.

Through this project, I have reinforced and expanded my knowledge of cloud computing, acquiring a clearer understanding of how to deploy and manage AWS services in a practical environment. At the same time, I developed skills in designing system architectures based on the principles of the AWS Well-Architected Framework, reading and understanding technical documentation, teamwork, project management, using Git/GitHub for source code control, as well as analyzing and resolving issues that arose during project development.

During my internship, I always took the initiative to study official AWS documentation and related technologies to supplement my knowledge when facing challenges. Furthermore, I regularly communicated with our mentor and team members to align on solutions, troubleshoot emerging issues, and complete assigned tasks on schedule. Although I still have much to learn and gain experience in, I always maintain a progressive mindset, actively welcome feedback, and continuously strive for self-improvement.

### Areas for Improvement

* **Continuously improve technical expertise** in cloud computing, Amazon Web Services (AWS) services, and related technologies to broaden my knowledge and practical implementation skills.

* **Enhance communication, discussion, and idea-presentation skills** during teamwork, as well as when reporting to the mentor and other team members to improve collaboration efficiency.

* **Proactively research and update new technologies and trends** in information technology, particularly Cloud Computing, DevOps, and Artificial Intelligence (AI), to better meet future enterprise demands.

Overall, the internship at the FIRST CLOUD AI JOURNEY (FCAJ) BOOTCAMP has been an incredibly meaningful and rewarding experience for me. The program provided the environment for me to apply what I have learned to real-world scenarios, while helping me accumulate professional knowledge, teamwork skills, problem-solving abilities, and a professional work ethic. The experiences gained throughout this internship will serve as a vital foundation for me to continue learning, growing, and preparing for my career after graduation.
