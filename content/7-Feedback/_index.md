During my participation in the BOOTCAMP FIRST CLOUD AI JOURNEY (FCAJ), I would like to share some personal feedback regarding my experiences as follows:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Learning and Working Environment**  
The learning environment at FIRST CLOUD AI JOURNEY (FCAJ) is very professional, friendly, and provides many opportunities for self-development. Although the program is organized as a Bootcamp, the way of working, progress management, and project execution are very similar to a real corporate environment. Mentors, Teaching Assistants (TAs), and fellow participants are always ready to support whenever I face difficulties, creating an open and positive learning atmosphere.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor always provides enthusiastic guidance, explains technical concepts clearly, and guides the approach to solutions instead of just giving answers. This helps me improve my self-research and problem-solving abilities. Additionally, the Team Admin always supports quickly regarding class schedules, documents, and program-related information, enabling me to focus on completing my assigned tasks effectively.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
As a Software Engineering student, I find the content of the program highly aligned with my learning and career orientation. The program helped me apply programming, software development, and teamwork knowledge to a real project, while gaining access to new technologies like Cloud Computing, AWS, and system deployment processes on cloud platforms. These are very useful pieces of knowledge and skills for my career after graduation.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During my time at the Bootcamp, I had the opportunity to learn a lot of new knowledge about AWS as well as real-world software development workflows. In addition to technical knowledge, I also honed skills such as teamwork, task management, using Git/GitHub, reading technical documentation, communication, and presenting ideas. These are all critical skills that make me more confident in my future studies and career path.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
What impressed me most at FCAJ was the spirit of sharing and mutual support among members. Everyone is always ready to share knowledge, help each other when facing difficulties, and work together to achieve project goals. The positive learning environment and collaborative spirit have helped me gain a wealth of experience and provided the motivation to continuously develop myself.

**6. Student Policies / Support**  
Although the program is not a traditional corporate internship model, FCAJ has provided excellent conditions for students through learning materials, mentor guiding sessions, specialized workshops, and opportunities to participate in real-world projects. These are very practical values that help students enhance their knowledge and accumulate experience before entering the job market.

---

### Additional Questions

* **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  What I found most satisfying was participating in a real project on the AWS platform, having the opportunity to apply what I learned to practice, and receiving enthusiastic guidance and support from the mentor and program members.

* **What do you think the program should improve for future students?**  
  In my opinion, the program could organize more sharing sessions with working industry engineers and introduce larger-scale scenarios or projects to give students more opportunities to experience real-world problems.

* **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they join FCAJ? Why?**  
  Yes. I would gladly recommend the FCAJ program to my friends because it is a high-quality learning environment with practical training content, dedicated mentors, and offers many opportunities for students to develop both technical knowledge and professional skills.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  I hope the program will organize more deep-dive practice sessions, workshops on new AWS technologies, and sharing sessions from industry experts to give students a more practical perspective on the job.

* **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
  Yes. If given the opportunity, I would love to continue participating in FCAJ's advanced training programs on Cloud Computing, DevOps, or AI to further enhance my technical knowledge and skills.

* **Any other comments (free sharing):**  
  I would like to express my gratitude to the Organizing Committee, Team Admin, mentors, and Teaching Assistants for always accompanying, supporting, and facilitating my successful completion of the program. The time spent at FCAJ has helped me accumulate a lot of practical knowledge, experience, and skills, while offering a clear direction for my future career path.
